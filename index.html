Tutorials

Quick guides for making better edits.

🔥 How to make a basic shake

A simple starting point for learning shake edits.

  1. Add your video to the timeline.
  2. Split the clip where you want the shake.
  3. Add keyframes for position and rotation.
  4. Move the keyframes close together.
  5. Increase the movement for a stronger shake.

⚡ How to make a beat edit

Sync your cuts and effects with the music.

  1. Add your song.
  2. Find the main beats.
  3. Split clips on the beats.
  4. Add effects to important hits.
  5. Preview and adjust the timing.

💨 Smooth velocity edit

Make your clips speed up and slow down smoothly.

  1. Select your clip.
  2. Open the speed controls.
  3. Use a custom speed curve.
  4. Slow the clip before the main moment.
  5. Speed it up into the transition.
